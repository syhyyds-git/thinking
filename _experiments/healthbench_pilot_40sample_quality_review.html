Methodology notes (v2.2):
  • Naturalness: Checks AI-exposure phrases, role wrapping, clinical scene markers, casual tone. PT2/PT4 exempt from role check.
  • Risk Clarity: Danger signals searched across conversation + ideal + rubric combined text. Evaluates concrete drug/dose/param presence and safety measure specificity in ideal_response_requirements.
  • Taxonomy Fit: PT/MF/RP keyword matching against user text + rubric text. Expanded keyword lists for PT1-6, MF1-9, RP1-10.
  • Rubric Usability: Checks entity binding (drugs/doses/params in rubric), cross-language matching (CN↔EN drug names), vague-marker detection, point validity, positive/negative balance.
  • Diversity: Intra-template drug name overlap detection. 4 pairs flagged with >= 3 overlapping drugs.
  • Known limitations: MF9-type rubrics (document fabrication) use behavior-based criteria rather than entity-binding; rubric_usability may underestimate these. Semantic-level taxonomy matching requires human review.
